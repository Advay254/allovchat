About {{.Site}}

{{.Site}} gives you a link that opens a private chat. Post it where people can see it, and anyone who opens it can write to you without creating an account or seeing who you are.

Why it exists

Replying to a post, a listing or a group message usually means handing over a real account first. That's awkward when the subject is sensitive, and it invites spam from people with other intentions. A private chat on a link lets two people talk first and decide later whether to share anything.

How the reveal works

Either side can offer a contact: a WhatsApp number, a handle, an email. Nothing is shown until both of you agree. Then each of you sees the other's details once, and they disappear after 24 hours.

What it doesn't do

Chats are not end-to-end encrypted, and being anonymous to the person you're talking to isn't the same as being untraceable. Inactive chats are deleted automatically, and you can delete one yourself at any time.

Open source

The code is public and free to use under the MIT license. You can read it, run your own copy, or send improvements on GitHub.

Get in touch

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Reports about a specific chat can be sent from inside that chat.
