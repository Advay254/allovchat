How it works

  1. 1

    Make a link

    Name it after the post or group it's for. Choose how long chats last, up to 7 days, and pause it whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Share it anywhere

    Guests type a message and send it. No sign-up. You see a request, and nothing reaches you until you accept it.

  3. 3

    Talk, then decide

    Chat as long as you like. If you both want to move to WhatsApp or anywhere else, you each agree and the details appear once.

Your identity. Your choice.

  • You approve every chat before it starts, and each person gets a fresh temporary name.
  • Leave any time with one tap. The chat disappears for both of you.
  • Turn on privacy mode to hide typing, read receipts and online status.
  • Contact details show only after you both say yes, and vanish after 24 hours.
  • Chats are encrypted in transit, not end-to-end, and every one is deleted within 7 days.
