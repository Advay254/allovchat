How it works

  1. 1

    Make a link

    Name it after the post or group it's for. You can have several, and pause any of them whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Share it anywhere

    Visitors type a message and send it. No sign-up. You see a request, and nothing reaches you until you accept it.

  3. 3

    Talk, then decide

    Chat as long as you like. If you both want to move to WhatsApp or anywhere else, you each agree and the details appear once.

Nobody gets your details by default

  • You approve every chat before it starts.
  • Block someone and they can't reach any of your links.
  • Contact details show only after you both say yes, and vanish after 24 hours.
  • Chats aren't end-to-end encrypted, and inactive ones are deleted automatically.
