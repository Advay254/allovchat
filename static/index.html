Chat anonymously. Reveal only when you trust each other.

Create your link

1. Create a link

Make one link per topic, post or community. Pause or delete it any time.

2. Share it anywhere

People who open it message you without an account. You accept or block each request.

3. Chat, then reveal

When you both agree, share a WhatsApp number or handle once. It disappears after 24 hours.

Built for privacy-minded people

Ask about a sensitive topic or product without handing strangers your real accounts. Visitors never sign up. You never see who they are. Nobody can spy on a profile you haven't shared.

Honest note: you're anonymous to the other person, not to whoever runs this server. Chats aren't end-to-end encrypted and old chats are deleted automatically.
