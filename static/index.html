How it works

Three steps, and you stay in control of every one.

  1. 1

    Make a link

    Name it after the post or group it is for. Choose how long chats last, up to 7 days, and pause it whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Share it anywhere

    Guests type a message and send it. No sign-up. You see a request, and nothing reaches you until you accept it.

  3. 3

    Talk, then decide

    Chat as long as you like. If you both want to move to WhatsApp or anywhere else, you each agree and the details appear once.

Your identity. Your choice.

Every part of AllovChat is built around one idea: you decide what other people learn about you.

You approve every chat

Each conversation starts as a request. Accept it, or block the guest in one tap.

A fresh name every time

Each person gets a new temporary name in every chat, so nothing links one conversation to the next.

End-to-end encrypted

Messages and shared contacts are encrypted in your browser. Our servers store ciphertext they cannot read, and a safety code lets you verify the keys.

Privacy mode, both ways

Hide your typing, read receipts and online status. It hides theirs from you too, so it is fair even if they have not turned it on.

Leave in one tap

The Leave button deletes the chat for both people at once. A guest lands on a neutral page, so nothing stays on screen.

Temporary by design

Chats last at most 7 days, with a warning 12 hours before deletion. Either person can end one sooner, and nobody can extend it.
