Scope

{{.Site}} lets people open a private chat from a link, without creating an account. This notice applies to the {{.Site}} website, the app, and every chat link created with it. It tells you what we handle when you use the service as a link owner or as a guest.

We ask for as little as possible. We never ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location.

Information we handle

Account information

Link owners choose a username and a password. The password is stored only as a salted, one-way hash, never in readable form. There is no email address or phone number on an account.

Link settings

The name of each link, plus any welcome message, about text, auto-reply, quiet hours, time zone and chat length you set for it.

Chat information

The temporary name each person is given, the encrypted messages, when they were sent, whether they were read (unless privacy mode hides it), the public encryption keys of both people, and the status of the chat. Message content and contact details are encrypted before they reach us.

Contact details you reveal

When both people agree to a reveal, the details each person typed are stored encrypted and removed 24 hours later.

Safety information

When an owner blocks a guest, we store a hashed device marker so the block holds across that owner's links. When you report a chat, we receive the reason, any note you add, and the messages you choose to attach.

Technical information

To deliver the service and protect it from abuse, our servers process your IP address and basic request details. Rate limits keep IP addresses in memory for a short time. Our hosting and database providers keep standard connection logs under their own schedules.

How we use it

We use information to run chats and deliver messages, to sign owners in and keep accounts secure, to enforce rate limits and stop abuse, to review safety reports, and to keep the service reliable and fix faults. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Encryption and what we can see

Messages and shared contact details are end-to-end encrypted. Your browser creates the keys, encrypts each message before sending, and the other person's browser decrypts it. Our servers only ever hold ciphertext, so we cannot read your conversations, and we cannot hand over readable messages to anyone.

We can see metadata: that a chat exists, when it started and ended, when messages were sent, roughly how large they are, the temporary names, and the names owners gave their links.

Your keys live in your browser. If you clear site data or switch device without your key backup, that chat cannot be opened again, and we cannot recover it for you. Each chat has a safety code you can compare with the other person to confirm that nobody sits in the middle.

Who we share information with

Infrastructure providers host the service and its database, and they process data on our behalf only to run it. When the human check is switched on, Cloudflare Turnstile loads on sign-in and registration to stop automated abuse, and sees what any site would see when you visit it.

We disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm. What we hold is limited, and the content of chats is encrypted, so we have no readable messages to disclose.

Cookies and local storage

We use only what the service needs to work. There are no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies.

  • Sign-in cookie. Keeps owners signed in. It is secure and not readable by scripts.
  • Chat cookies. Let a guest return to their chat, and let an owner block a guest.
  • Local storage. Holds your encryption keys and your preferences, such as privacy mode by default, alerts and haptics. It stays on your device.

Because these are essential to the service, no cookie banner is needed.

Your controls

  • Leave a chat. The Leave button deletes the chat for both people immediately.
  • End a chat sooner. Either person can shorten a chat. Nobody can extend it.
  • Privacy mode. Hides your typing, read receipts and online status, and hides the other person's from you.
  • Block and report. Block a guest in one tap, or report a chat to our team.
  • Delete a link. Removes the link and every chat on it.

Delete your account

Open Settings and choose Delete account. You confirm twice, then enter your password and type DELETE. Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your account, your sign-in sessions, your block list, every link you made and every chat on those links, for both people. Chats you saved to your account from other people's links are removed from it, and the other person's copy follows its normal schedule. Nobody, including us, can bring a deleted account back.

Safety reports

Sending a report is your choice. We receive the reason, your note, and only the messages you attach. In an encrypted chat we cannot see anything you do not send. We use reports to protect people and to enforce our terms, and a report is deleted with its chat.

How we protect information

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Messages and contact details are end-to-end encrypted.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed, and sign-in sessions can be revoked on the server.
  • A strict content security policy and modern security headers protect every page.
  • Rate limits and a human check protect sign-in and chat creation from abuse.
  • The code is open source, so anyone can review it.

To report a vulnerability, follow the security policy.

Children

{{.Site}} is for adults. People under 18 may not use it. If we learn that an account or chat involves someone under 18, we close it.

Changes to this notice

When we change this notice, we update the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is handled, we announce it on the site.
